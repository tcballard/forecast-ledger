# Counting rules — source summary

Source: https://ballotbench.com/methodology, captured 28 October 2026. These are research notes, not a substitute for checking current official rules.

- **Louisiana House (LA-1 through LA-6):** November 3 uses an all-candidate ballot. Winning a majority elects the candidate; otherwise the top two advance to December 12. Forecast the eventual seat winner. First-round vote shares are a different question. The source identifies Louisiana's 2022 map as applicable.
- **Alaska Senate, governor and House:** Forecast final victory after any ranked-choice eliminations, not merely the first-choice leader. The source says the RCV count is scheduled for November 18.
- **Maine Senate and House district 2:** The source identifies declared write-ins and ranked-choice counting if no candidate clears the relevant first-round majority threshold. Forecast the eventual winner.
- **Ballot measures and retention:** Verify the measure's substance, approval threshold and any legal challenge. Yes means adopted or retained; passage need not equal a simple majority.
